Extending the Election Hotelling Model
Game Theory and Information, final report · In Chinese · PDF

Added party reputation and policy tolerance as parameters to the classical Hotelling model, then analyzed whether pure-strategy Nash equilibrium exists under different candidate utility functions and cost structures. Most configurations have none - which may be why we see such diverse platforms in real-world elections.
